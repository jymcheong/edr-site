Architect of CyberSniper.

Twenty years in endpoint security, government authentication federations and CSOC management, now poured into one end-to-end system.

I designed and built CyberSniper from the ground up: an agentic cyber deception platform that runs on infrastructure you own.

Deception changes the economics of defense. Real users never touch a decoy, so when something does, it is an attack, not a maybe. No flood of alerts to triage, no guessing. Intruders spend their effort in the wrong places while the real assets stay out of reach. AI agents then do the investigating that normally needs a full SOC, so a small team gets clear verdicts instead of a backlog.

edr.sg is where I publish what that work proves in the field: what to deploy, how to configure it, and why.
